    The problem

    Gyms train parts.
    You move as a whole.

    Machines and isolated lifts work in two dimensions. Your spine, hips and feet work in three. That gap is where pain, injury and plateaus live.

    At TR we assess your posture and gait first, release what's restricted, then rebuild integrated, three-dimensional movement under load. It's challenging for your brain as much as your body.
