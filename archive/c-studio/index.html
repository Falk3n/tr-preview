Your first visit

Gentle start. Real answers.

Most clients arrive with pain they've had for years. Your first session is about understanding why.

  1. A conversation

    Your history, goals and what's been holding you back.

  2. Posture & gait

    We photograph your posture and watch you walk. You'll see what we see.

  3. Release & reset

    Simple self-massage and corrective moves you can feel working straight away.

  4. Your plan

    A program built for you, in the TR app with video of every exercise.
