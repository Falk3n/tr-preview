01 · The method

Assess. Correct. Integrate. Perform.

Traditional programs train isolated muscles in two dimensions. Your body doesn't move that way. Every TR client follows the same four-stage process, paced to what your body shows us.

01

Assess

Static posture photos, gait analysis and a movement screen. We find what's been restricting you, often for years.

02

Correct

Self-myofascial release and corrective patterns restore pelvic position, breathing and core control.

03

Integrate

Three-dimensional, full-body patterns that connect feet to fingertips. Hard on the brain as well as the body.

04

Perform

Load, speed and running mechanics, built on a structure that can take it. Strength that lasts decades.

Conventional gym training
