How it would connect

The current site runs on Wix with Bookings, Members, Pricing Plans, Online Programs and Store. Wix now offers headless access, so a new website and a TR app can share the same bookings, member logins and plans. Clients log in once, and calendars stay in sync without migrating data.

PieceProposalWhy
WebsiteNew custom front end, with Wix kept as the engine (bookings, members, payments)Full design freedom without losing existing clients, packs or history
Client appOne iPhone + Android app (React Native / Expo)Same login as the website; sessions pulled live from bookings
Exercise videosDarren films each exercise once and it's streamed from a video CDNInstant start on mobile; roughly 200 clips cost a few dollars a month to stream
Session plansDarren picks exercises per client per session from his libraryShows on the client's phone instantly (see the app's "Darren's view")
